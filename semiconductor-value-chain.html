Digital

Binary signals (0 / 1). Scales with Moore's Law — drives leading-edge capex.

Memory — stores data

DRAM

Volatile. Loses data when power is lost.

NAND Flash

Non-volatile. Slower, cheaper per bit.

Logic — computes and directs

GPU

Processes many tasks at once. Well suited for graphics and AI.

CPU

Gives directions to other chips. Processes one task at a time.

ASIC

Designed for one specific purpose. AI accelerators, for example.

FPGA

Can be reprogrammed after manufacturing.

Integration

SoC — System on Chip

Combines other chips onto one chip. Includes memory, CPU and GPU.

Analog

Continuous signals — temperature, sound, light, power. Mature nodes, long product lives, high margins.

Core categories

Signal

Measures real-world signals and converts them to data.

Power

Manages power in electronics: switches, battery management.

Adjacent categories

MCU

Small controller with CPU + memory + I/O on one die.

RF

Radio front-end for 5G, Wi-Fi and satellite links.

Sensor / MEMS

Image sensors, accelerometers, microphones.

Compound semiconductors

SiC

High-voltage power for EV traction and grid.

GaN

High-frequency power for chargers and RF.
